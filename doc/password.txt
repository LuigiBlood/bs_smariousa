$80B8B9 - Routine to generate base episode password

$80B8A5 - Gold Mario Statues Max per episode (always 10)
$80B8AF - Seed? per episode

$198A = $1958 (16-bit) - Total Gold Marios for the episode
$198E = $1962 + $115422 + $115424 + $115426 + $115428 (16-bit)
$1992 = $196E (16-bit) - Total Score

$80B3C1 - Tiles to use for Passwords (based from $7E1860)

$7E1810 - Tile Map Password
$7E1830 - Satellaview Serial Number
	$1842 = Second to Last Digit of Satellaview S/N (ASCII, repeated twice)
	$1844 = Last Digit of Satellaview S/N (ASCII, repeated twice)
	$1846 = Checksum Byte of Satellaview EEPROM (repeated twice)

$7E1860 - RAW Password
- 0x00 - If maxed out Gold Marios: 0x0A | (Episode Number << 4) (0x0A, 0x1A, 0x2A, 0x3A)
       - If not:                   0x0028,0x001F,0x000F,0x002F (depending on Episode)
	   - Used for EOR
- 0x02 - ($1993 & 0x03F0) >> 4
- 0x04 - ($1992 & 0x0FC0) >> 6
- 0x06 - ($1992 & 0x003F)
- 0x08 - ($198F & 0x03F0) >> 4
- 0x0A - ($198E & 0x0FC0) >> 6
- 0x0C - ($198E & 0x003F)
- 0x0E - ((($1846 & 0x000F) | (($1844 & 0x000F) << 4) | (($1842 & 0x000F) << 8)) & 0x0FC0) >> 6
       - Used for EOR
- 0x10 - (($1846 & 0x000F) | (($1844 & 0x000F) << 4) | (($1842 & 0x000F) << 8)) & 0x003F
       - Used for EOR

Then
0x02 = 0x02 EOR 0x00 EOR 0x0E
0x04 = 0x04 EOR 0x00 EOR 0x10
0x06 = 0x06 EOR 0x00 EOR 0x0E
0x08 = 0x08 EOR 0x00 EOR 0x10
0x0A = 0x0A EOR 0x00 EOR 0x0E
0x0C = 0x0C EOR 0x00 EOR 0x10
